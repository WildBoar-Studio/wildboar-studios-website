Delete your Foody.ai account and data

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Foody.ai is made by Wild Boar Studios. You can delete your Foody.ai account and its data at any time, from inside the app or by contacting us.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Foody.ai and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap App settings.
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete account and confirm.

This takes effect immediately and also clears your profile, pantry and price overrides from the device.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email support@wildboarstudios.io with the subject "Foody.ai account deletion request". If you signed in with Google or Apple, send it from (or include) the email address of that account so we can find it. We will confirm once your data has been deleted, normally within 30 days.

If you never signed in, your account is tied only to an anonymous identifier on your device, so we cannot look it up by email. Use the in-app option above, or reinstall the app and use it.

What is deleted

  • Your account and any linked Google or Apple sign-in
  • Your saved recipe catalogue, including notes, ratings and tags
  • Recipes generated for your account, along with the preferences (such as allergies and diets) stored with them
  • Your sign-in sessions on every device

What may be kept

  • A usage ledger (which AI feature was used, an approximate cost and a timestamp) with your account identifier removed, kept for billing and abuse prevention.
  • Purchase and subscription records held by Google, Apple and our purchase provider (RevenueCat), as required for payments, refunds and tax.
  • Crash reports already sent to our error-monitoring provider (Sentry), which expire automatically.

For more detail, see our privacy policy.
