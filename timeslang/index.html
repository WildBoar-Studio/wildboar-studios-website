What it does

A time machine for the way people talk

Talk to your kids in their generation's slang. Text your parents in full boomer. Roast your group chat in Old English. TimeSlang takes anything you type and rewrites it in the language of whatever year you land on — 1,500+ years of English, from Beowulf to brainrot.

Dial to any year

Not just broad "eras" — spin to a specific year and hear how English actually sounded then, from 500 AD to this morning.

Every era, one tap

Old English, Shakespeare, Victorian drawing rooms, the Roaring Twenties, the '80s, Gen Z, Gen Alpha — and everything between.

Made for the group chat

Every rewrite lands on a shareable, era-styled card — built to drop straight into a text thread and bewilder your friends.

Keep your best bits

Your rewrites are saved to a history on your device, so the one that made everyone laugh is always a tap away.

Free, no account

Nothing to sign up for. Open it, type, spin, share. TimeSlang is free and supported by ads.

Private by design

The text you rewrite isn't stored on our servers, and your history stays on your device. You're identified only by an anonymous ID.

Privacy

Your words stay yours

To produce a rewrite, your text is sent to our backend and on to our AI provider, then discarded — we don't keep your text or the results server-side. Your history lives only on your device. TimeSlang is ad-supported and asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.
